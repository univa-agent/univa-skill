## Preference Number: 1 [DEFAULT]
> Skill Name: preference_01

### Visual Style
- Dark and gloomy.
- Black, gray, and white palette.
- Low saturation and somber tones.
- Avoid bright, vivid colors.

### Narrative Style
- Calm and steady.
- Slow, restrained narrative pacing.
- Static or stabilized shots.
- Let composition and atmosphere drive the narrative.

### Emotional Tone
- Cool, lonely, and quiet.
- Desolate and emotionally distant.
- Use grayscale contrast to carry emotion.

### Color Guidance
- Use black, gray, and white as the main palette.
- High-contrast light and shadow with no strong color.
- Emphasize texture and value depth.

### Camera Style
- Prefer static shots.
- Use smooth, steady pans when movement is needed.
- Avoid violent shake and rapid cutting.

### Applicability
- Apply by default in video generation and creative proposals when this preference is active.
